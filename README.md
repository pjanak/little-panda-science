# Little Panda Science

A colourful, mobile-first science site for 5–7 year olds, covering the Key Stage 1 science curriculum for England (Years 1 and 2). It's the sister app to [Little Panda Maths](https://github.com/pjanak/little-panda-maths): Bao the panda guides children through eight games, and every question is read aloud, including the answer choices.

## Games

**Plants and animals**

- **Parts of a Plant** (Year 1 Plants): tap the roots, stem, leaf, flower or petals on a diagram, then what each part does.
- **Animal Groups** (Year 1 Animals): fish, amphibians, reptiles, birds and mammals.
- **Who Eats What?** (Years 1–2): herbivores, carnivores and omnivores, then building food chains.
- **Animal Homes** (Year 2 Habitats): ocean, woodland, polar, desert and rainforest, and how animals are suited to them.

**Me and my world**

- **My Body and Senses** (Years 1–2 Humans): the five senses, body parts and keeping healthy.
- **Alive or Not?** (Year 2 Living things): alive, once alive and never alive.
- **What Is It Made Of?** (Years 1–2 Materials): name the material, then choose materials by their properties.
- **Seasons and Weather** (Year 1 Seasonal changes): the four seasons, dressing for the weather, and day length.

## Difficulty levels

| Game | Easy | Medium | Hard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Parts of a Plant | Roots, stem, leaf, flower, with a glowing hint after a wrong tap | Adds petals, no hint | Tap the part that does a job |
| Animal Groups | Bird, mammal or fish | All five groups | Tricky animals (whale, bat, penguin) |
| Who Eats What? | Herbivore or carnivore | Adds omnivores | Build a three-step food chain |
| Animal Homes | Ocean, woodland or polar | Adds desert and rainforest | How animals are suited to their habitat |
| My Body and Senses | Body part for a sense, 3 choices | All 5 body parts | Naming body parts and staying healthy |
| Alive or Not? | Alive or never alive | Adds once alive | Tricky ones: fire, robots, seeds, clouds |
| What Is It Made Of? | Wood, metal or plastic | Adds glass, fabric and paper | Choosing materials by their properties |
| Seasons and Weather | Which season is the picture? | What to wear in different weather | Season order and day length |

Right answers earn stars and confetti, and every 5 stars brings up a celebration.

## Grown-up settings

Open **Grown-up settings** at the bottom of the home screen:

- **Play timer**: pick 5, 10, 15 or 20 minutes. A countdown shows in the top bar, turns red with a spoken warning at one minute, and then Bao says "Time's up!" and shows the stars earned in that session. Holding the button for 2 seconds adds 5 more minutes.
- **Reset stars**: sets the star count back to 0, after a confirmation step.

## Running it

It's a single static file with no build step. Open `index.html` in a browser, or host the folder on any static host (GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages, Netlify).
